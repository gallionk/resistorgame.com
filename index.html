RESISTOR

A team-based online robot shooter with a 1980s cartoon look. Grab a weapon, pick your cover and fight for your side.

BLUE RED

The game

Team battles

Blue robots against red in fast team deathmatch. Join a match already in progress; bots fill any empty spots.

Arsenal

Handguns, SMGs and assault rifles, grenades, weapon crates to break open and a resupply station worth fighting over.

Bots with a plan

Computer players take cover, flank, climb ladders, lob grenades at campers - and a few of them are very large thugs.

Hand-built maps

Warehouses with walkways and ladder towers, spawn rooms behind energy shields, and a city street at dusk.
